The Housing We Need

“The future is already here, and it’s beautiful.”

TypeGreen paper
Our roleResearch, narrative & design
CollaboratorsBPIE · VELUX · RISE · Artelia

A green paper and systems framework on how the building industry can answer four crises at once: climate and biodiversity breakdown, energy and resource insecurity, unaffordable housing, and unhealthy homes. For the first time, the paper treats them as one system.

The findings went further than expected. By using Europe’s existing buildings better, the industry could unlock 50 to 107 million additional homes, house up to 250 million more people, avoid up to 19 billion tonnes of CO₂, and prevent up to 13 billion tonnes of material use.

The point is simple. The building industry is not only part of the problem. It could become one of our strongest responses to it — a sector moving from cause to cure.

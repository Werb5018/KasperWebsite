Beyond the Roadmap

“A transition plan for the Danish building industry.”

TypeRoadmap
Our roleResearch & framework
CollaboratorsEFFEKT · CEBRA · Artelia

Beyond the Roadmap is a transition plan for the Danish building industry, answering a simple but difficult question: what comes after reduction, when a sector is already operating beyond planetary limits?

At its centre is the Butterfly Framework — a method for benchmarking progress against absolute climate and ecological limits. It combines sufficiency and impact reduction with active restoration, shifting the ambition from doing less harm to rebuilding the natural systems the industry depends on.

The result is not a fixed recipe, but a destination and a framework for getting there.

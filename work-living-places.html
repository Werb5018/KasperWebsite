Living Places

“A new way of thinking about building.”

TypeRoadmap
Our roleStrategy & communication
CollaboratorsVELUX · Artelia · Enemærk & Petersen · EFFEKT

Living Places demonstrates how homes can be built with 60–75% lower emissions and a threefold improvement in indoor environmental quality — all at market price. At the time, it achieved the lowest measured building emissions ever recorded.

The project combines advanced construction methods, low-emission materials and a systemic approach designed to ensure economic viability and scalability. The full methodology was released open source and published as a book.

It serves as a blueprint for systemic change, proving that major environmental and health improvements are possible within existing economic frameworks.

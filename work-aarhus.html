Aarhus Climate Budget Tool

“The global carbon budget, as an everyday building tool.”

TypeTool
Our roleTool design & data visualisation
CollaboratorsArtelia · Aarhus Kommune

Denmark’s first decision-making tool that links municipal construction policy directly to a CO₂ budget. It turns climate science into a live governance mechanism, not a retrospective footnote.

The tool treats carbon like money: a limited resource to be defined, allocated and tracked across the entire building portfolio. Every project — new build or renovation — can be measured against Paris-aligned pathways.

By operationalising the global carbon budget at the municipal level, it brings climate science into the rooms where decisions actually happen, and sets a scalable model for climate-conscious governance.

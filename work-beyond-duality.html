Beyond Duality

“Unlearning modernity, remembering ecology.”

TypeTool
Our roleCourse design & narrative
CollaboratorsErin Remblance

Beyond Duality is an eight-week learning course for people who want to understand the systems shaping our world, and gain the agency to change them. It has already been taken by some of the leading pioneers working in sustainability and systems change.

This is not a course in sustainability literacy. It is a process of unlearning modernity and remembering ecology — examining the assumptions that shape how we understand nature, economy, progress and ourselves.

When you understand the system you are part of, you can navigate it differently, make better decisions, and intervene where change can actually happen.

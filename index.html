[ About us ]

A non-profit research and design agency. Not a think tank, not a creative studio, not a campaign shop — the infrastructure that connects all three.

Purpose

The world doesn’t lack awareness. It lacks agency. We exist to close the gap between knowing and acting.

Mission

We transform complex, real-world challenges into responses that empower collective action, and help organisations navigate the necessary transition.

Vision

A post-growth, sufficiency-based and equitable world. A better world for all — not a slightly greener version of this one.

What we stand for

  • Physical reality over wishful thinking
  • Sufficiency before efficiency before renewables
  • Open knowledge over owned knowledge
  • Distributed agency over centralised solutions
“Organisations hire us when they have knowledge nobody acts on.”

[ Work ]

Not a theory of change. A way of working that has already proven its impact.

Green paper BPIE · VELUX · RISE · Artelia

The Housing We Need, for the Future We Want

“The future is already here, and it’s beautiful.”

Treating climate, biodiversity, resource insecurity and unhealthy homes as one system — and showing that better use of Europe’s existing buildings could unlock up to 107 million homes and avoid up to 19 billion tonnes of CO₂.

Roadmap VELUX

Re:Living

Restoring the building industry, one existing home at a time.

The first model designed to guide the European building industry back within core system boundaries — built on the People and Planet Model, uniting Earth System Science and Building Biology.

Tool Artelia · Aarhus Kommune

Aarhus Climate Budget Tool

“Turning the global carbon budget into a tool for everyday building decisions.”

Denmark’s first decision-making tool linking municipal construction policy directly to a CO₂ budget — treating carbon like money, governed across a whole portfolio, ahead of time.

Movement EFFEKT · CEBRA · Artelia · 900+ companies

Reduction Roadmap

“Mobilizing the Danish building industry.”

The first time an entire industry came together not to fight regulation but to demand it — more than 900 companies signing on for a legally binding, science-aligned carbon cap.

Roadmap EFFEKT · CEBRA · Artelia

Beyond the Roadmap

“A transition plan for the Danish building industry.”

Asking what comes after reduction — the Butterfly Framework benchmarks progress against absolute climate and ecological limits, shifting ambition from doing less harm to active restoration.

Roadmap VELUX · Artelia · Enemærk & Petersen · EFFEKT

Living Places

“A new way of thinking about building.”

Homes built with 60–75% lower emissions and a threefold improvement in indoor quality — at market price. The lowest measured building emissions ever recorded at the time, released open source.

Tool Erin Remblance

Beyond Duality

“Unlearning modernity, remembering ecology.”

An eight-week course for people who want to understand the systems shaping our world — and gain the agency to change them. A process of moving from sensing something is wrong to knowing how to act on it.

[ Services ]

You have the research. The numbers hold. So why is nothing happening? We offer four ways in.

01

The Green Paper / Report

Turn your knowledge into a public agenda.

An agenda-setting publication and the campaign around it — the narrative, the visuals, the launch, the press strategy, the political framing.

For foundations, NGOs, industry organisations, companies and research institutions.

02

The Roadmap

Find out what your organisation should actually do.

A strategic roadmap grounded in physical reality — concrete decisions, thresholds and sequences, plus the internal narrative that gets your own people on board.

For businesses, municipalities, NGOs and institutions sensing their strategy won’t survive the next decade.

03

The Tool & Prototype

Put physical reality into the room where decisions are made.

Decision-making tools that translate planetary boundaries into budgets, scenarios and thresholds — and prototypes that prove the ambitious choice can be built.

For municipalities, public asset owners, industry organisations and NGOs.

04

The Movement

Turn agreement into action.

Campaigns that convert passive support into visible, repeatable collective action — the identity, the assets, the coalition, the moments that make joining feel natural.

For NGOs, alliances, grassroots movements and anyone who needs critical mass, not just an audience.

Why a non-profit? We’re legally prohibited from extracting profit. Our advice isn’t shaped by upselling — our incentive is your impact, because impact is the only thing we’re allowed to accumulate.

[ About us ]

A non-profit research and design agency.

Not a think tank, not a creative studio, not a campaign shop — the infrastructure that connects all three, because change dies in the gaps between them.

The world doesn’t lack awareness. It lacks agency. We exist to close the gap between knowing and acting.

Purpose

People already know the system is failing. What they lack is a future they can see themselves in, and a way to help build it. We turn minority insight into majority action.

Mission

We transform complex, real-world challenges into responses that empower collective action, and help organisations navigate the necessary transition.

Vision

A post-growth, sufficiency-based and equitable world. A better world for all — not a slightly greener version of this one.

What we stand for

  • Physical reality over wishful thinking
  • Sufficiency before efficiency before renewables
  • Open knowledge over owned knowledge
  • Distributed agency over centralised solutions

What we do

  • Turn research and insight into public agendas that shape debate and policy
  • Build transition roadmaps that make organisations resilient within planetary boundaries
  • Design decision-making tools that translate climate science into governance
  • Design campaigns and coalitions that convert agreement into action

What we don’t

  • Help greenwash, or dress expansion up as transition
  • Do growth marketing
  • Produce communication that isn’t anchored in research that holds
  • Sell silver bullets
“Organisations hire us when they have knowledge nobody acts on.”

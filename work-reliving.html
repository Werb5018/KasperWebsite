Re:Living

“Restoring the building industry, one existing home at a time.”

TypeRoadmap
Our roleModel & framework design
CollaboratorsVELUX

Re:Living is an ongoing initiative to transform the building industry from a source of environmental degradation into a net-positive, restorative force. It introduces the first model designed to guide the European building industry back within core system boundaries while enhancing wellbeing for all.

At its heart is the People and Planet Model — a framework that brings together Earth System Science, which defines the absolute environmental limits required to keep the planet stable, and Building Biology, which defines the conditions people need to thrive inside buildings.

Re:Living proves that our existing building stock can shift from being part of the problem to becoming a powerful solution for planetary health and human flourishing.

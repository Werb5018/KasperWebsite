Reduction Roadmap

“Mobilizing the Danish building industry.”

TypeMovement
Our roleCampaign & mobilisation
CollaboratorsEFFEKT · CEBRA · Artelia · 900+ companies

The Reduction Roadmap is a research project and industry-wide mobilisation campaign that proved real change is possible when we enable collective action. It started with a number: the emissions limit Danish construction must stay within to align with a 1.5°C carbon budget.

Then it turned that number into a movement. More than 900 companies signed on — the vast majority of the Danish building industry — to demand binding regulation. It was the first time in Danish history an entire industry came together not to fight regulation but to demand it.

A determined minority mobilised a whole sector, and that sector made systemic change impossible to ignore.
